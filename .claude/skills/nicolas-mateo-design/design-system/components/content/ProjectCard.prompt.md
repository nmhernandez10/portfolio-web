One-line: the work grid unit — media band, role/period meta row, outcome sentence, mono metrics, stack tags.

```jsx
<ProjectCard
  title="Keel Mind"
  role="Feature Architect"
  period="2022 — now"
  summary="A regulated mental-health platform: session pipeline, notifications and reporting."
  metrics={["200k+ sessions", "85k+ clients"]}
  stack={["NestJS", "PostgreSQL", "AWS Lambda"]}
/>
```

- Leave `media` off until a real screenshot exists; the empty band says so in mono rather than faking a mockup.
- The arrow glyph nudges up-right on hover — the whole card is the link.
