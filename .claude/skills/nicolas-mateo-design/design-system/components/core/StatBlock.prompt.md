One-line: a display-size number with a mono caption — used in rows of three or four to make scale concrete.

```jsx
<StatBlock value="200k+" label="Sessions delivered" />
<StatBlock value="6+" label="Years shipping" size="lg" />
```

- Numbers are always real and drawn from the résumé; never round up for looks.
- Group in a flex row with --space-12 gaps, separated by hairlines rather than cards.
